#### **Part 1: Meetings & Discussions (会议与讨论)**

这个部分是万能的，适用于任何会议。

- **阐述会议目标 (Stating the goal):**
    
    - "The main goal of this meeting is to..." (本次会议的主要目标是...)
        
        - _Example:_ "...to **align on** the Q4 roadmap for our social VR app."
            
    - "What I'd like to accomplish today is..." (我今天希望完成的是...)
        
        - _Example:_ "...to **decide on** the core mechanics for the new hand-tracking game."
            
    - "We're here today to discuss..." (我们今天在这里讨论...)
        
        - _Example:_ "...the user feedback from our latest passthrough feature."
            
- **发表个人观点 (Giving your opinion):**
    
    - "From my perspective,..." (从我的角度来看...)
        
        - _Example:_ "...the biggest risk is the **latency**."
            
    - "I believe we should..." (我相信我们应该...)
        
        - _Example:_ "...**prioritize** the onboarding flow improvement."
            
    - "What I'm thinking is..." (我的想法是...)
        
        - _Example:_ "...we could **leverage** spatial audio to make the experience more immersive."
            
- **引导讨论 (Guiding the conversation):**
    
    - "That's a great point. Let's also consider..." (那一点很好。我们再考虑一下...)
        
    - "Let's stay focused on [topic]. We can discuss [other topic] later." (我们先专注于[话题A]。[话题B]我们可以稍后讨论。)
        
    - "To move forward, we need to make a decision on..." (为了推进下去，我们需要就...做出决定。)
        
- **总结与下一步 (Summarizing & Next Steps):**
    
    - "So, to recap, we've agreed on..." (总结一下，我们达成了一致...)
        
        - _Example:_ "...A, B, and C."
            
    - "The key takeaways are..." (关键的结论是...)
        
    - "The next steps are: [Person A] will..., and [Person B] will..." (下一步是：[A]将负责...，[B]将负责...)
        

#### **Part 2: Presenting Your Product (阐述产品)**

在产品评审、立项会等场合使用。

- **定义问题 (Defining the problem):**
    
    - "The problem we're trying to solve is..." (我们想要解决的问题是...)
        
        - _Example:_ "...that new users feel isolated when they first enter our virtual world."
            
    - "Our users are struggling with..." (我们的用户在...方面感到困难。)
        
        - _Example:_ "...the current gesture controls, which are not intuitive."
            
- **阐述愿景与价值 (Explaining the vision & value):**
    
    - "We envision a future where..." (我们设想的未来是...)
        
        - _Example:_ "...remote collaboration feels as natural as being in the same room."
            
    - "This feature will allow our users to..." (这个功能将允许我们的用户...)
        
        - _Example:_ "...co-create 3D models in real-time."
            
    - "The core value proposition is..." (核心价值主张是...)
        
        - _Example:_ "...**simplicity and immersion**."
            
- **介绍方案 (Introducing the solution):**
    
    - "Our proposed solution is..." (我们提议的解决方案是...)
        
        - _Example:_ "...a shared virtual workspace with persistent objects."
            persistent  /pər'sɪstənt/：持续不断的
    - "Here's how it works. First, the user..." (它的工作原理是这样。首先，用户...)
        
- **谈论目标与成功指标 (Talking about goals & success metrics):**
    
    - "Our primary goal is to..." (我们的主要目标是...)
        
        - _Example:_ "...**increase user retention** by 15%."
            retention: 留存 % percent
    - "We'll measure success by looking at..." (我们将通过观察...来衡量成功。)
        
        - _Example:_ "...the **daily active users (DAU)** and the **average session length**."
            

#### **Part 3: Working with Engineers (与工程师协作)**

在需求评审、日常同步时使用。

- **描述用户故事 (Describing a user story):**
    
    - "As a [user type], I want to [action], so that [benefit]." (作为一个[用户类型]，我想要[做什么]，以便[获得什么好处]。)
        
        - _Example:_ "**As a** new user, **I want to** see a quick tutorial *on*  gestures, **so that** I can interact with the environment immediately."
            
- **定义需求与范围 (Defining requirements & scope):**
    
    - "The minimum viable product (MVP) for this feature should include..." (这个功能的 MVP 应该包括...)
        /'mɪnɪməm/
        viable 可行的
    - "For V1, we need to support..." (在第一个版本，我们需要支持...)
        
        - _Example:_ "...at least three types of virtual objects."
            
    - "This is a 'must-have', while that is a 'nice-to-have'." (这个是“必须有”，而那个是“有了更好”。)
        
- **讨论技术问题 (Discussing technical issues):**
    
    - "What are the technical constraints we should be aware of?" (我们需要注意哪些技术限制？)
        
    - "How feasible is it to implement...?" (实现...的技术可行性如何？)
        feasible=可行的，可能的，可执行的  implement vt. 使生效, 贯彻, 执行
    - "Let's talk about the trade-offs between [Option A] and [Option B]." (我们来讨论一下方案A和方案B之间的利弊/权衡。)
        

#### **Part 4: Working with Designers (与设计师协作)**

在设计启动会、评审设计稿和讨论用户体验时使用。

- **启动设计 (Kicking off design):**
    
    - "I've put together a PRD for the new [feature]. Let's walk through the user problem together." (我为新功能写好了产品需求文档。我们一起来过一下用户问题。)
        
    - "Could you explore some initial concepts for...?" (你可以为...探索一些初步的概念吗？)
        
        - _Example:_ "...how users might interact with 3D objects from a distance."
            
    - "The key thing to keep in mind is..." (需要牢记的关键点是...)
        
        - _Example:_ "...we need to minimize the risk of **motion sickness**."
            
- **提供反馈 (Giving feedback):**
    
    - "This is a great start. I especially like..." (这是一个很好的开始。我特别喜欢...)
        
        - _Example:_ "...the way you've organized the virtual menu."
            
    - "I have a question about the user flow. From a user's perspective, this step might feel..." (关于用户流程我有个问题。从用户的角度来看，这一步可能会感觉...)
        
        - _Example:_ "...a bit **confusing**."
            
    - "How does this design solve for [a specific problem]?" (这个设计如何解决[某个特定问题]？)
        
        - _Example:_ "...the issue of text **legibility** at different distances?"
            
- **讨论用户体验 (Discussing UX):**
    
    - "Let's think about the **first-time user experience (FTUE)**. Is it intuitive enough?" (我们来考虑一下首次用户体验。它足够直观吗？)
        
    - "How can we make this interaction feel more **natural and responsive**?" (我们如何让这个交互感觉更自然、响应更及时？)
        
    - "We should consider using **haptics** or **spatial audio** to provide better feedback to the user." (我们应该考虑使用触觉反馈或空间音频，来给用户提供更好的反馈。)
        
- **讨论视觉与美学 (Discussing visuals & aesthetics):**
    
    - "The overall visual style should feel..." (整体的视觉风格应该感觉...)
        
        - _Example:_ "...**futuristic but also friendly**."
            
    - "Could we explore a different color palette? This one feels a bit..." (我们能探索一下不同的色板吗？这个感觉有点...)
        
        - _Example:_ "...too dark for a social app."
            

**如何练习：**

1. **不要贪多：** 每周专注于一个部分。例如，这周只练习“会议与讨论”部分。
    
2. **造句练习：** 把你实际工作中的内容，套进这些句式里。比如，用 "The problem we're trying to solve is..." 来描述你手头正在做的项目。
    
3. **会议前准备：** 在开会前，预测你会用到哪些句式，提前写下来，这就是你的“剧本”。
    

坚持下去，你会发现自己不再是“满脑子单词”，而是“满脑子模块”，可以根据需要随时调用组合，流畅地表达你的专业思想。
