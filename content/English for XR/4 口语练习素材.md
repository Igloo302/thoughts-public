### **场景一：The Kick-off Meeting (开场定位)**

**背景:** 这是你和 Google Android XR 团队的第一次正式会议。他们对 OST 眼镜有基本了解，但不够深入。

**Google PM 问:**

> "Welcome! We're excited to collaborate. To start, could you walk us through your perspective? What are the most critical differences between designing for  OST glasses like XREAL's, and designing for fully immersive VR headsets that we're more familiar with?"

**回答思路框架 (Framework to Answer):**

- **1. Acknowledge and Frame:** (感谢并确立你的专家角色)
    
    - _"That's a great question, and it's really the core of our discussion."_
        
    - _"From our perspective as OST practitioners..."_
        
- **2. Highlight Key Differentiator #1: Context is King** (核心差异1：与现实世界的结合)
    
    - _VR is about **world replacement**. OST is about **world enhancement**._
        
    - _Our users are not in a virtual space; they are in the **real world**, walking, working, and talking to people._
        
    - _This means every digital object is a guest in their reality. It cannot be intrusive._
        
- **3. Highlight Key Differentiator #2: Information Hierarchy** (核心差异2：信息呈现方式)
    
    - _In VR, you can have large dashboards. In OST, the **Field of View (FOV) is limited**._
        
    - _We must be extremely selective about **what** information to show, **when** to show it, and **where** to place it._
        
    - _Think 'glanceable' information, not 'immersive' panels._
        
- **4. Conclude with an Implication:** (总结并引出对 Android XR 的要求)
    
    - _"So, this means we need a version of Android XR that is fundamentally more aware of the user's real-world context and prioritizes subtlety and efficiency."_

### **场景二：The Notification System (具体功能：通知)**

**背景:** 你们正在深入讨论核心系统功能。设计师对如何在 OST 眼镜上处理通知感到担忧。

**Google Designer 问:**

> "Let's talk about notifications. On a phone, they can be distracting. On OST glasses, they could be downright dangerous if they block the user's view. How should the Android XR system handle notifications in a 'see-through' context?"

**回答思路框架 (Framework to Answer):**

- **1. Agree and State the Principle:** (同意并陈述设计原则)
    
    - _"You've hit on a crucial point. Our guiding principle should be: **'inform, don't interrupt'.**"_
        
- **2. Propose a Tiered System (Tier 1 - Ambient):** (提出分层系统 - 环境感知层)
    
    - _For low-priority notifications, we shouldn't show any text._
        
    - _Imagine a **subtle glow** in the periphery of your vision, or a soft, **spatial audio cue** from the direction of your phone._
        
    - _The user is simply **aware** that something is new, but not forced to look._
        
- **3. Propose a Tiered System (Tier 2 - Glanceable):** (提出分层系统 - 可瞥视层)
    
    - _For medium-priority notifications, like a text message, we could show a **small icon or a single line of text** in a non-critical area._
        
    - _The user can **glance** at it, but it disappears after a few seconds._
        
- **4. Propose a Tiered System (Tier 3 - Actionable):** (提出分层系统 - 可交互层)
    
    - _Only for high-priority notifications, like an incoming call or navigation instruction, should we present an **actionable UI**._
        
    - _And even then, the system must be **context-aware**. If the user is driving or in a conversation, it should hold the notification._
        
- **5. Conclude with a Suggestion for Google:**
    
    - _"So, we suggest that Android XR needs a **'Notification Priority and Context API'** that allows apps to define the urgency and the system to decide the presentation."_

### **场景三：The Input Method Debate (核心交互：输入方式)**

**背景:** Google 的 PM 倾向于将他们在 VR 中成熟的手部追踪方案直接应用过来。你需要提出 OST 领域的不同见解。

**Google PM 问:**

> "Our data shows that hand tracking is the most intuitive input method for spatial computing. We think we should make it the primary input for Android XR on glasses. What are your thoughts?"

**回答思路框架 (Framework to Answer):**

- **1. Validate their Point, then Pivot:** (肯定对方观点，然后转折)
    
    - _"I agree that for immersive experiences, hand tracking is fantastic. However, for all-day OST glasses, the **social context and power consumption** present unique challenges."_
        
- **2. Raise the "Social Acceptability" Issue:** (提出“社会接受度”问题)
    
    - _Users will be wearing our glasses in public, on a subway, or in an office._
        
    - _Performing large hand gestures is **socially awkward** and fatiguing over time._
        
    - _We need something more **discreet and subtle** for common interactions._
        
- **3. Propose a Hybrid Approach:** (提出混合方案)
    
    - _We see a multi-modal approach. For primary navigation—scrolling, clicking—a **low-energy input device** like a ring or even a connected phone is more practical._
        
    - _Hand tracking should be reserved for **specific, high-value interactions**, like manipulating a 3D model or playing a game, where the user has opted into that experience._
        
- **4. Make a Concrete Suggestion:** (给出具体建议)
    
    - _"Therefore, we recommend that the Android XR input framework should be built around a **primary 'discreet' input** while allowing apps to call for **'expressive' hand-tracking modes** when needed."_

### **场景四：The Developer's Perspective (平台生态：开发者)**

**背景:** 会议转向了如何吸引开发者。Google 想知道如何让现有的手机 App 开发者轻松过渡。

**Google PM 问:**

> "To build a successful ecosystem, we need apps. From your standpoint, what are the top 2-3 things the Android XR platform must provide to make it incredibly easy for existing Android mobile developers to adapt their apps for OST glasses?"

**回答思路框架 (Framework to Answer):**

- **1. State the Core Challenge:** (陈述核心挑战)
    
    - _"The biggest mental shift for mobile developers is moving from a **2D screen to a 3D world** where the 'screen' is unbounded."_
        
- **2. Suggestion #1: Smart UI Components:** (建议1：智能UI组件)
    
    - _First, we need **"XR-native UI components"**. A developer shouldn't have to learn a 3D engine like Unity just to display a button._
        
    - _Imagine an API where a developer can just say 'display this notification at a 2-meter distance, locked to the user's view' and the system handles all the 3D rendering._
        
- **3. Suggestion #2: Contextual Data APIs:** (建议2：情境数据API)
    
    - _Second, developers need easy access to **contextual signals**. Is the user sitting? Walking? Looking at a specific object?_
        
    - _An easy-to-use **'Context API'** would allow apps to be truly smart. For example, a map app could automatically show directions when it detects the user is walking._
        
- **4. Summarize the Value Proposition:** (总结价值主张)
    
    - _"If we provide these two things—**easy UI and powerful context**—developers won't need to rebuild their apps. They can simply **extend their existing mobile apps** with new, magical OST features."_

### **场景五：The "Pushback" Scenario (高阶：应对反对意见)**

**背景:** 你提出了一个 XREAL 认为很重要的定制化需求，但这与 Google 强调平台一致性的理念相悖。

**Google Designer 反驳:**

> "We understand your point about needing a custom launcher for OST. But we're concerned this will fragment the Android XR experience. A consistent user interface is crucial for our platform's identity. Why is this level of customization absolutely necessary?"

**回答思路框架 (Framework to Answer):**

- **1. Empathize and Find Common Ground:** (共情并找到共同点)
    
    - _"I completely understand the need for a consistent platform identity. We share that goal. A predictable experience is better for all users."_
        
- **2. Reframe the Problem:** (重新定义问题)
    
    - _"The issue isn't about branding or preference; it's about a **fundamentally different use case** that demands a different interaction paradigm."_
        
    - _"A phone's app grid is designed for a **'destination-based'** task. You decide to open an app. An OST launcher should be **'context-based'**. It should surface information relevant to your current situation."_
        
- **3. Give a Concrete Example:** (给出一个具体例子)
    
    - _Imagine a user looking at a restaurant. The launcher shouldn't present a grid of 30 apps. It should proactively offer **one or two relevant options**: 'View Menu' or 'Make Reservation'._
        
    - _This requires a launcher that is deeply integrated with the perception system, which goes beyond the standard app grid._
        
- **4. Propose a Collaborative Solution:** (提出合作共赢的方案)
    
    - _"Perhaps the solution isn't a completely 'custom' launcher, but rather we could **co-design a new 'contextual launcher module' for the core Android XR system**. This would benefit all OST partners in the future while maintaining a consistent design language from Google."_
        
    - _"This way, we are not breaking consistency; we are **evolving it for a new category of devices**."_
